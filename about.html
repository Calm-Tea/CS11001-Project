Fun Facts about Thomas the Tank Engine

Real-Life Basis

Thomas is based on the London, Brighton and South Coast Railway 0-6-0 E2 with extended side tanks.

Narrator Legends

The TV series has been narrated by famous figures including Ringo Starr and George Carlin.

The Island of Sodor

All the adventures take place on the fictional Island of Sodor, located between the Isle of Man and Cumbria.

Fact #1: Thomas is a steam engine, not a diesel! He is known for being a "Really Useful Engine."

Fact #2: He was originally a wooden toy carved by Rev. W. Awdry for his son, Christopher.

Fact #3: The first book, 'The Three Railway Engines', didn't actually feature Thomas; he was introduced in the second book.

Niche Information

The "Christmas" Origin

Thomas wasn't actually in the very first book. He was created as a Christmas gift in 1942 when Reverend W. Awdry carved a wooden tank engine toy for his son, Christopher, who was recovering from the measles.

The Ghost Writer

While the Reverend wrote the original 26 books, his son Christopher Awdry eventually took over the series in 1983 to keep the stories going for a new generation, adding 16 more books to the 'Railway Series' canon.
