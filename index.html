Who is Thomas the Tank Engine?

Thomas is a blue anthropomorphic soft-hearted steam engine who lives on the Island of Sodor. He first appeared in The Railway Series books by Reverend W. Awdry.

The Real-Life Thomas

Thomas the Tank Engine was inspired by the LB&SCR E2 class steam locomotive (shown above). These 0-6-0 tank engines were built between 1913 and 1916 for shunting duties in London.
